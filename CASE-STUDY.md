## Project snapshot

A multi-page website for a café that turns into a fire-cooked restaurant after 5 pm, built to win orders and bookings back from delivery and booking apps by making ordering and reserving faster on its own site.

## Project overview

Independent cafés and restaurants lose 25 to 35% of every delivery-app order in commission, and most of their own websites are a menu PDF and a phone number. Ember needed a site that felt as warm as the room, and that let a guest order a coffee or book dinner in under a minute.

Because Ember is two businesses in one (a bright brunch café by day, a candlelit grill by night), the site has two moods. It picks one from the time of day, and a sun/moon switch flips everything by hand: colours, hero photo, headline, featured dishes and the default menu section all change as the new mood spreads out of the switch in a circle.

From there, every path ends in revenue: the menu adds straight to a pickup or delivery order, a drink builder lets guests design their cup and send it to the bar, and a three-step booking flow ends with a calendar invite. An AI host answers questions and can start a booking from a single sentence.

## Key features

1. **Day ↔ Night mode**: one switch changes the whole site between café and kitchen.
2. **Living hero**: steam by day and smoke by night drift over the photo and follow the pointer.
3. **Build your cup**: choose base, milk, size and sweetness, and watch the glass pour your drink.
4. **Online ordering**: filters for vegan, gluten-free and spicy, quick view with allergens, cart, checkout and a live order tracker.
5. **Table reservations**: available times, seating with photos, and a ticket with Add to calendar.
6. **AI host**: understands "table for 4 tomorrow at 7pm" and opens the booking ready to confirm.
7. **Easy to rebrand**: the name, hours, menu and copy all live in one file.

## Results to mention (for a real client)

- Orders and bookings stay on the business's own site, with no commission.
- One site markets both the brunch trade and the dinner trade.
- Mobile-first: every flow works with a thumb, from menu to confirmation.
